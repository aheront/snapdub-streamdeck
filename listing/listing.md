## Step 2: Details

Name (can't be changed later):
SnapDub

Price: Free

Description:
Turn your stream into short vertical clips with one key.

Copy a link to your stream or video and press Make clips. SnapDub finds the best moments, frames them for vertical, adds captions and makes clips ready for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. Works with YouTube, Twitch, Kick, Rumble and Zoom recordings.

The key shows the progress while you keep streaming. When the clips are ready, press it again and they open in your browser, where you can edit, download or post them.

Keys
Make clips: press with a link copied, or set a fixed link in the key settings. Choose how many clips you want and one of your saved templates.
Credits: shows how many credits you have left. Press it to see your plan.

Getting started
Open the settings of either key and press Sign in with SnapDub. Your browser opens, you approve, and the keys are ready. A free account works.
